# Phase 7 Surface Texture Provenance

> Updated: 2026-08-24
> Scope: Phase 7 Wallpaper and Wainscoting production candidates

## Source and use-right status

All four source images in this folder were generated for AnimalCafe with OpenAI ImageGen during the Phase 7 design session. They do not copy the commercial wallpaper reference images previously reviewed; those commercial images were visual-direction references only and were not imported into the project.

The generated source images are retained outside Unity `Assets/` in this folder. Production copies are stored under `Assets/Art/Phase7/Textures/` and imported by Unity at a maximum size of `512 × 512`.

Studio Owner visual acceptance remains separate from technical import and repeat validation.
